Unsubscribe from FishLogic email

Every marketing email we send has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom, and most mail apps also show an Unsubscribe button next to the sender name. Either one takes effect immediately.

If you no longer have the message, email support@fishlogicapp.com with the subject "Unsubscribe" from the address you want removed. We honor every request within 10 business days, usually the same day.

Unsubscribing does not change your FishLogic account or the push notifications you have turned on in the app; those are controlled in the app's Settings.
